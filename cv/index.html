Email biotech.pankaj@gmail.com

Summary Postdoctoral scholar developing vascularized organ-on-chip disease models that integrate mechanobiology, biomaterials, stem cell engineering, single-cell sequencing, and spatial omics. My research program focuses on how the cellular microenvironment, extracellular matrix composition, matrix mechanics, and tissue architecture regulate stem cell maintenance, expansion, differentiation, and disease-relevant phenotypes. I aim to build clinically relevant microphysiological models for therapeutic discovery, regenerative medicine, and mechanistic studies of development and disease.
